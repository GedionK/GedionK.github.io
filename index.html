Privacy Policy - ምን አዲስ (Min Addis) News Clipper

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how ምን አዲስ (Min Addis) ("we", "us") handles information in News Clipper, the software we use to produce and publish our own Amharic news videos about Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa. News Clipper is an internal tool: it is used only by our own team to publish to our own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. It is not offered to the public, and members of the public do not sign in to it.

1. Who we are

  • Name: ምን አዲስ (Min Addis)
  • Website: https://gedionk.github.io
  • Contact: gedionkebede2010@gmail.com

2. What information we use

a. Our team's accounts in News Clipper. For each team member: name, e-mail address, role, a password stored only as a secure hash, and a log of their actions in the tool (for accountability).

b. Our own platform accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok). When we connect one of our channels, the platform gives News Clipper an access token after we sign in on the platform's own page and agree. We use it only to:

  • upload our own news videos to our own channel or page, with their title, description and tags;
  • read the information needed for that (for example the channel or page name, and the link of the published video).

We do not read, collect or store the personal data of viewers, subscribers, commenters or other users of these platforms. We do not read private messages.

c. News sources. To write news videos we read public news articles and feeds. These are public information about events, not personal data collected from users.

3. How we store and protect it

  • Platform tokens and API keys are stored encrypted on our own server and are never shown in the tool after they are entered.
  • Access to News Clipper needs a personal login over HTTPS; every action is logged.
  • We keep backups of our server, encrypted, for disaster recovery.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone. Video files and their texts are sent only to the platform we publish to, at our request. Our AI writing assistant (Anthropic's Claude API) receives the public news text it summarises; it receives no platform tokens and no viewer data.

5. How long we keep it

  • Platform tokens: until we disconnect the channel in News Clipper or revoke access on the platform.
  • Team accounts and action logs: while the person is on the team, then up to 12 months.
  • Published videos stay on the platforms until we remove them there.

6. Your choices and removing access

  • You can revoke News Clipper's access to a Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions , to Facebook/Instagram in their Settings → Apps and websites, and to TikTok in Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. We also disconnect channels inside News Clipper.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to gedionkebede2010@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

7. Google API Services (YouTube)

News Clipper uses the YouTube API Services. By using our channel content you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

News Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We request only the scope needed to upload our own videos (youtube.upload), use the data only for that purpose, do not transfer it to others, do not use it for advertising, and do not let people read it except where needed for security, to comply with the law, or with our explicit consent.

8. Meta and TikTok

For Facebook and Instagram we follow the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies; for TikTok the TikTok Developer Terms of Service. Data from these platforms is used only to publish our own videos to our own page or account, as described above.

9. Children

News Clipper is not meant for children and does not knowingly process children's data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: gedionkebede2010@gmail.com
